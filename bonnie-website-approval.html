Five things only you can answer

These are the last open decisions. They are quick, and each one is holding something up.

1. What should the site be called in Google?

Your current site is titled "Cosmetic Injections Melbourne | Cosmetic Injectables Treatment". That is the wording you flagged as a worry, and you are right to. The new site says "Bonnie Lass Beauty | Cosmetic Nurse and Skin Clinic, Melton". If you would rather it read "Skin and Wellness Clinic", or something else, say so here.

2. Your before and after photos

The private gallery is built and locked, but it is holding placeholder images until your real ones arrive. We need pairs shot at the same angle in the same light, and written consent from each client. Tell us roughly how many you have and when you can send them.

3. Gift vouchers

The site currently says to ask in clinic or call. If you would like a buy-online link instead, send us the link and we will put it on the site.

4. The Seasons font

Your brand document names The Seasons for headings. It is a licensed font, so we cannot buy or download it for you. If you have it in your Canva account, send us the file and we will swap it in. Until then the site uses Cormorant Garamond, which is very close.

5. Navy and gold, or the softer green?

The site is built in the navy and gold from your logo. Your brand document lists a softer sage green and blue instead. We went with the logo because that is what your clients already recognise. Tell us if you would rather the green.
