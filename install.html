Install Your Claude

One command sets up Claude and every Clinic Catalyst skill on your computer. Copy, paste, and let it run.

Before you start
  • Laptop plugged in and on good wifi. It takes about 25 to 45 minutes.
  • A paid Claude plan (Pro or Max). Make sure you can log in at claude.ai.
  • Your Mac login password handy (the one you use to unlock your laptop).
The rhythm every time: hit Copy, click into the window, paste, press Enter. You never type the commands yourself.

1 Open the Terminal

Hold Command and tap Spacebar. Type Terminal. Press Enter.
A plain window opens. That's where everything goes.

2 Paste the install, press Enter

curl -fsSL https://clinic-catalyst-au.github.io/cc-onboard/clinic-bootstrap.sh | bash
Paste with Command + V, then press Enter.
Asked for a password? Type your Mac login password and press Enter. Nothing shows while you type. That's normal.
Says "Press RETURN"? Just press Enter.

3 Let it run

Leave the window open and go make a cuppa. Lots of text scrolls past. That's it working.
You're finished when it says DONE - everything installed and verified.
If it says DONE WITH PROBLEMS, take a screenshot of the window and send it to us.

4 Close and reopen the Terminal

Press Command + Q to fully quit the Terminal, then open it again (step 1). This switches everything on.

5 Start Claude in your Clinic folder

cd ~/Clinic && claude
Paste, press Enter. The first time, Claude asks you to sign in: choose your Claude account, a browser opens, click Authorize, then come back to the Terminal.

1 Open PowerShell

Click Start, type PowerShell, and click Windows PowerShell to open it.
Open it the normal way. Don't choose "Run as administrator".

2 Paste the install, press Enter

irm https://clinic-catalyst-au.github.io/cc-onboard/clinic-bootstrap.ps1 | iex
Paste with Ctrl + V (or right-click), then press Enter.
Windows asks "Do you want to allow this app to make changes?" Click Yes. It may ask a few times.

3 Let it run

Leave the window open and go make a cuppa. Lots of text scrolls past. That's it working.
You're finished when the list at the end shows ok next to each item.
If you see MISSING, take a screenshot of the window and send it to us.

4 Close and reopen PowerShell

Close the PowerShell window completely, then open it again (step 1). This switches everything on.

5 Start Claude in your Clinic folder

cd ~\Clinic; claude
Paste, press Enter. The first time, Claude asks you to sign in: choose your Claude account, a browser opens, click Authorize, then come back to PowerShell.

6 Teach Claude your clinic

Inside Claude, type this and press Enter. It asks you questions about your clinic, your clients and how you talk.
/cc-resonance

7 Set up your brand

When that's finished, run this one. It captures your colours, fonts and look so everything Claude makes looks like you.
/cc-brand-guide

You're in 🎉

Claude now knows your clinic and has every Clinic Catalyst skill ready to go.

Next time, just open the Terminal (or PowerShell) and run the step 5 command.
